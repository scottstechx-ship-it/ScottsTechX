Examinations and assessments

Assessment runs throughout each term so that parents see progress before the final examination.

Beginning-of-term test (BOT)Weeks 2 – 3 of each termOn the term report card
Mid-of-term test (MOT)Weeks 5 – 6 of each termOn the term report card
End-of-term examinationLast two weeks of each termReport card at the end of term
UCE (S.4) and UACE (S.6)October – NovemberUNEB releases results nationally

Term fees

Fees are payable at the beginning of each term and no later than the 15th day of the term. The school office issues a receipt for every payment, and statements can be requested at any time.

Please note: term dates may change if the Ministry of Education and Sports revises the national calendar, or in the event of an emergency. Any change is announced on this page, on the news page and through the parent portal — please check back at the start of every term. Learners should report by 8:00am on the opening day.
