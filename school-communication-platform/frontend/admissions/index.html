ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Who Can Apply?

S.1 Intake

Senior One (New Students)

  • Completed Primary Leaving Examination (PLE)
  • Age between 12–14 years by intake year
  • Passed PLE with minimum score of 250 marks
  • Successfully completed entrance examination
  • Attended interview with parent/guardian
  • Submitted all required documents
S.2 – S.4

Transfer Students (O-Level)

  • Passed previous class with minimum Division 3
  • Valid UCE results slip (for S.4 transfers)
  • Transfer letter from previous school
  • Good conduct certificate from previous school
  • Passed school entrance assessment
  • Interview with parent/guardian
S.5 – S.6

Advanced Level (A-Level)

  • Passed UCE with minimum 8 subjects
  • Minimum aggregate of 16 points in best 8 subjects
  • Passed at least 2 science subjects for Science combination
  • Interview with student and parent/guardian
  • Showed commitment to A-Level curriculum
