ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS

Who Can Apply?

S.1 Intake

Senior One (New Students)

  • Completed Primary Leaving Examination (PLE)
  • PLE results slip for the year of application
  • Entry requirements and cut-off as set by the school for that intake
  • Attended interview with parent/guardian
  • Submitted all required documents
S.2 – S.4

Transfer Students (O-Level)

  • Reports from the previous class showing satisfactory progress
  • Valid results slip where examinations have been sat
  • Transfer letter from previous school
  • Good conduct certificate from previous school
  • Passed school entrance assessment
  • Interview with parent/guardian
S.5 – S.6

Advanced Level (A-Level)

  • UCE results slip showing passes in the subjects to be continued
  • Grades that meet the school's A-Level entry requirement for the chosen combination
  • Passed at least 2 science subjects for Science combination
  • Interview with student and parent/guardian
  • Showed commitment to A-Level curriculum
ACADEMIC PROGRAMS

What We Offer

Kalinabiri SS offers a comprehensive curriculum at both O-Level and A-Level, blending core academics, sciences, and applied subjects to equip every learner.
