OUR STORY

A Legacy Built Since 1965

Kalinabiri Secondary School was founded in 1965 on the historic Kalinabiri hill, overlooking the scenic Busoga region near Lake Victoria. What began as a modest community secondary school has grown into one of Uganda's most respected educational institutions.

Named after the hill that shelters our campus, Kalinabiri has for 59 years served as a beacon of hope and opportunity for young people across the region. Our school was established with a clear mission: to provide quality education that develops the whole person — academically, morally, and socially.

In the early years, the school operated with just three classrooms and a dedicated staff of five teachers. Today, Kalinabiri SS boasts modern infrastructure, over 60 qualified teachers, and more than 1,200 students from diverse backgrounds across Uganda.

Throughout our history, we have remained committed to our core values of discipline, excellence, integrity, and community service. These values, instilled in every student who passes through our gates, have produced generations of graduates who contribute meaningfully to Ugandan society and the world at large.

OUR CAMPUS

World-Class Facilities

Our campus provides everything students need for academic success, personal growth, and holistic development.

🍽️

Dining Hall

Serves a balanced midday meal for students and staff, with a menu developed alongside a qualified nutritionist.

🔬

Science Laboratories

3 fully-equipped labs: Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Each accommodates 40 students simultaneously with individual workstation setups.

💻

Computer Labs

2 labs with 100+ modern computers, internet access, and specialized software for ICT and programming classes.

📚

Library

10,000+ volumes, digital research stations, e-library access, study carrels, and a dedicated quiet reading room.

⚽

Sports Complex

Full-size football pitch, basketball court, netball courts, volleyball court, and athletics track. Regular inter-class and inter-school competitions.

🎵

Music & Art Studios

Dedicated rooms for music practice (instruments available), art exhibitions, drama productions, and creative writing sessions.

⛪

School Chapel

Inter-denominational chapel for spiritual gatherings, assemblies, and quiet reflection. Hosts weekly services and special events.

🏥

Medical Clinic

First aid trained staff on duty daily. Sick bay with 6 beds. Referral system to Jinja Regional Referral Hospital for serious cases.

🔧

Technical Workshop

Woodwork and metalwork workshop for practical vocational training. Agriculture demonstration garden for hands-on learning.

📡

ICT Innovation Hub

Robotics, coding clubs, 3D printing, and digital innovation programs. Students compete in national technology competitions.

🛡️

Campus Security

Perimeter wall, 24/7 security guards, CCTV surveillance, controlled gate access. Safe and secure learning environment guaranteed.
