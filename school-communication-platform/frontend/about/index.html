OUR STORY

A Legacy Built Since 1984

Kalinabiri Secondary School is a government-aided, mixed day and boarding secondary school in Ntinda, Nakawa Division, Kampala. It was established by the Government of Uganda in 1984 on the grounds of Kalinabiri Primary School.

The school opened with just eleven pioneer students, who were taught under the trees within the primary school compound while the young school waited for a home of its own. In 1985, Mr. Ernest Kayanja donated 3.82 acres of land to the school, and a further 0.59 acres were purchased, giving Kalinabiri SS a permanent campus of its own.

From those first eleven learners, the school has grown into a full secondary school offering both O-Level (S.1–S.4) and A-Level (S.5–S.6) education to between 800 and 1,000 students each year, drawn from Ntinda and across Uganda.

Our motto, “Determined To Excel With Trust In God,” guides everything we do. The school is supported in its work by development partners including the Center for the Girl Child, Compassion International and Agape, who help us keep vulnerable learners in school and strengthen our guidance and welfare programmes.

Science at Kalinabiri is hands-on: our students build and demonstrate their own apparatus with locally available materials — from thermometers and simple circuits to magnetism and heat experiments — because we believe learners understand best what they can touch, make and explain.

OUR CAMPUS

World-Class Facilities

Our campus provides everything students need for academic success, personal growth, and holistic development.

Dining Hall

Serves a balanced midday meal for students and staff, with a menu developed alongside a qualified nutritionist.

Science Laboratories

3 fully-equipped labs: Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Each accommodates 40 students simultaneously with individual workstation setups.

Computer Labs

2 labs with 100+ modern computers, internet access, and specialized software for ICT and programming classes.

Library

10,000+ volumes, digital research stations, e-library access, study carrels, and a dedicated quiet reading room.

Sports Complex

Full-size football pitch, basketball court, netball courts, volleyball court, and athletics track. Regular inter-class and inter-school competitions.

Music & Art Studios

Dedicated rooms for music practice (instruments available), art exhibitions, drama productions, and creative writing sessions.

School Chapel

Inter-denominational chapel for spiritual gatherings, assemblies, and quiet reflection. Hosts weekly services and special events.

Medical Clinic

First aid trained staff on duty daily. Sick bay with 6 beds. Referral system to Jinja Regional Referral Hospital for serious cases.

Technical Workshop

Woodwork and metalwork workshop for practical vocational training. Agriculture demonstration garden for hands-on learning.

ICT Innovation Hub

Robotics, coding clubs, 3D printing, and digital innovation programs. Students compete in national technology competitions.

Campus Security

Perimeter wall, 24/7 security guards, CCTV surveillance, controlled gate access. Safe and secure learning environment guaranteed.
