What every A-Level candidate takes

Alongside the three principal subjects of the combination, each learner takes the subjects below. These are timetabled by the school and examined by UNEB at the end of S.6.

ComponentWho takes itPurpose
Three principal subjectsAll learnersThe combination chosen at entry to S.5
Subsidiary Mathematics or Subsidiary ICTAll learnersNumeracy and computing skills required for university entry
General PaperAll learnersReasoning, comprehension and essay writing across disciplines
Practical workScience combinationsLaboratory skills assessed both internally and by UNEB

Entry requirements

Places in S.5 are offered on the basis of UCE results, the combination applied for and the availability of places. The requirements below are the school's normal minimum; the final decision rests with the Director of Studies and the Head Teacher.

Science combinations

  • UCE with passes in Mathematics and at least two sciences
  • Credit standard in the subjects of the chosen combination
  • For PCM, BCM and PEM: a solid Mathematics grade is essential
  • For BCFN and BCA: a pass in the relevant optional subject is an advantage

Arts & humanities combinations

  • UCE with a pass in Mathematics and English
  • Credits in the subjects of the combination where available
  • MEG, MEE: a pass in Mathematics is required to cope with the course
  • BCG: a pass in at least one science subject

Documents to bring

  • UCE result slip (and school pass slip where available)
  • P.L.E. and UCE index numbers
  • Birth certificate or notification of birth
  • Church / mosque or baptism card, as applicable
  • Two passport-size photographs

How to join A-Level here

Apply

Fill in the online application form on the admissions page, or collect a form from the school office after UCE results are released.

Submit your results

Attach or present your UCE result slip together with the documents listed above.

Subject guidance

The Director of Studies reviews your results against your chosen combination and advises on alternatives where needed.

Admission and reporting

Once admitted, you are placed in an S.5 class, issued the A-Level requirements list and told the reporting date.

Applications for S.5 are open

Talk to the school office or apply online — day places are available.
